How to Choose the Best Website Development Company in Kashmir

In Kashmir it is very difficult to find a proper website development company. Numerous web developers and agencies promise attractive websites, employ different techniques and propose different prices. Some of these developers can offer you a standard website at low prices, while others suggest an expensive website with the latest features.

The hard part is figuring out what you really need and whether the person or company you are hiring can do it right.

A website is not just another expense for a business. This is often the first place that a potential client comes to view your services, view your work, read about your business and make the decision whether they should contact you. A poorly designed or slow-loading website can leave a bad impression. However, a well-designed website can make a small business appear professional and trustworthy.

If you are looking for a web design company in Kashmir, here are few practical things worth checking before you make your decision.

Start with your own requirements

Before contacting a developer, think over your requirements.

A local shop may have different needs than a hotel, an online store may have nothing in common with a school. A simple shop may require only a few pages for describing the products, with contacts and address. A hotel may want to have information about their rooms, a booking system and inquiry form. A school may request to post some information about admissions and results with notices. An online store may need something more substantial, usually a full-scale e-commerce platform with a product catalog, payment system and other services. Needless to say, these are all different tasks of varying degrees of complexity.

Therefore, it is unreasonable to expect to be able to design and develop all of them for the lowest possible price and using some off-the-shelf CMS. A developer worth their salt will first ask questions to understand what you need, and then recommend you a suitable technology stack and features set to meet your specific goals.

Look at their previous work

One of the easiest ways to understand what a developer can do is to take a look at sites they have made.

Instead of just looking at their portfolios, try to access the sites themselves if possible.

Use your phone and the computer they have to look through them and see if everything works fine.

Take a particular interest in the websites' navigation and overall design.

Check different kinds of businesses and see what they have done: sites for portfolio, shops, schools, restaurants, and local businesses. This way, you will get an idea of the developer's versatility. The point is not in the quantity; several neat examples will be more helpful than dozens of average ones.

Don't ignore the mobile experience

This thing is the first question that a lot of people may have when launching their site on the web.

Opening the website on your phone do you see the text on the screen comfortably? Are buttons easy to press? Is the menu comfortable? Are images displayed at a comfortable size? Are forms filled out without having to zoom in and out?

A website that looks good but is uncomfortable to use on a mobile device defeats its own purpose.

When hiring a company to create a website in Jammu and Kashmir, ask them how they intend to ensure that the website has been tested and optimized on different screen sizes. It is important to remember that responsive design should be an integral part of the design process as opposed to something that is added at the end.

Ask how SEO will be handled

You do not need to be an SEO expert yourself before hiring a web developer. Still, you need to ask yourself whether SEO was considered while creating the website

For example, make sure the developer knows how to create clean URL’s, SEO-friendly page structure, headings, mobile-friendly, crawlable website.

XML-sitemap(robot.txt, canonical, HTTPS, structured data), etc., may also be relevant for your website.

Most importantly, the website needs to have a good structure for the user and the search engines.

For example, when offering website development, SEO, and digital marketing services, you should not leave everything in one large text block on your homepage. A proper structure would be to have separate pages for each service, describing exactly what it entails.

Website speed matters

Have you ever been on a website with your phone and decided not to wait for it to load?

Most people are not patient enough to wait for a website to load on their phones

There are many things that slow down a website, things like your host, images, coding, scripts and much more.

When talking to your developer about your project ask them what they plan on doing to keep your website sleek and fast.

Images that are not optimized, excess scripts, and bad coding can turn an aesthetically pleasing website into a nightmare. After your website is launched you can use online tools such as Google Pagespeed Insights to test and determine what you can improve on. The last thing you want is for someone to go to your website and be greeted with a slow loading nightmare.

Your website should be beautiful, yes, but most importantly it should be quick and responsive.

Choose technology based on the project

You may stumble upon such terms as WordPress, PHP, React, Next.js, Shopify, WooCommerce, etc.

It is easy to get confused thinking that the latest technology is the best one. In most cases it is not the case.

A small business website may be developed using WordPress, while an online store may be built on Shopify or WooCommerce. A custom application may take a different approach and use a completely different tech stack.

The wrong question to ask is: "Which technology is the best?".

Instead, you should be asking:

"Which technology is appropriate for what I need?"

A good website developer should be able to explain this in simple language instead of making the decision sound unnecessarily complicated.

Think about future changes

Your website is unlikely to remain stagnant for the entirety of its existence.

New services may be added, articles published, new projects uploaded, contact information changed, or products added at some point.

It is essential to consider your future requirements and ask yourself how easy or hard it will be to make the necessary changes.

For instance, with a WordPress-based website, you might update some elements on your own, while an application will always require a developer in order to make any changes.

Both are not necessarily bad options; the key point is to have an idea of what future maintenance you may have to deal with.

Security should be part of the project

Website security is another area that is easy to overlook.

At a minimum websites should be using HTTPS and be hosted/configured properly.

If the website deals with users accounts, forms, payments or customer information, website security is even more important.

Ask The developer how you can ensure that backups, updates, authentication and sensitive information are safe.

For an e-commerce site, payment processing should be handled by an appropriate secure payment processor rather than storing unnecessary sensitive information.

You don't need to know all of the technical details for securing a website, but a responsible web developer will be able to tell you what they are.

Don't make your decision only on price

Price is, of course, a critical factor in any business relationship.

However, it is vital to distinguish between “finding an affordable website” and “looking for the cheapest option available.”

“If this web designer says, his rates start at ₹7000 or another one asks for ₹ 25000, which one is in a better position to offer me a good value service?”

That is a difficult question to answer, because it depends on what exactly you are being offered.

You need to specify – in this or that deal, what services and products are included in the price.

For instance – does the price you are being offered include website design, hosting, domain name, contact forms, SEO, responsive design, security, content upload, maintenance, or further support services?

The cheapest option sometimes has the costliest aftermath.

So, you need to ask your questions carefully.

“Would you please provide me with the list of services included?” Instead of asking “How much does the website cost?”

Local knowledge can be useful

If your business is concentrated on catering to the inhabitants of Kashmir, working with someone who knows the territory and its specifics will benefit you.

Such a specialist will understand the audience and know how to build the working strategies and specific business plan.

For instance, if your online store delivers goods to Srinagar, its promotion strategy will differ significantly from the one used by a similar business that operates nationwide.

However, this does not mean that you should choose only local services; technical expertise and professionalism remain important factors.

Thus, regional knowledge and experience in SEO and web development are valuable qualities to look for in a service provider.

Ask about support after launch

The day your site goes live is rarely the end of the project

You may find that you need to tweak a contact form, change a word or two, update a plugin, or that your hosting service has had an outage

Ask your developer about what happens after launch

Some offer a certain amount of technical support as part of the project, whereas others bill hourly for any maintenance work

The bottom line is that there should be no confusion on who is responsible for what, and who will bill you if something goes wrong

After all, you want your website to continue to look amazing and function perfectly, long after the initial launch.

Communication matters more than people think

A technically strong developer can be very hard to work with.

Ask yourself what you noticed during your initial talks with them.

Did they answer your questions clearly?

Were they responsive to your requirements?

Did they give you honest timelines?

Did they know when something was unnecessary?

Communication is very important during development.

You don't want to waste time trying to understand what a developer is saying.

A good developer will sometimes tell you that an idea you want simply won't work, or that there is a better way to do it.

That is a part of professional advice.

Final thoughts

Choosing a website development company in Kashmir boils down to understanding the balance between expertise, communication, budget and support.

Avoid choosing a company on the basis of lowest price and avoid choosing on the basis of a particular programming language.

Check their portfolio and test their websites, ask them about their mobile friendly design, SEO, security and support options and make sure you understand what you are paying for and that you can actually access your digital assets.

Most importantly, think of your website as a business asset rather than a one time design project

A well built website can help your business by helping customers understand what you offer, helping them find you through search engines, helping them contact you and helping them trust you.

Whether you are a local business owner in Srinagar, an online retailer, a school, a hotel, a start up or a professional service provider, you should think about choosing the right website development company in Kashmir as it will save you a lot of headaches in the future.

The best website development company in Kashmir for you, will be the one that understands your needs, creates a website that serves your customers and supports you long after launch.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost in Kashmir?

The quote of a website in Kashmir is primarily driven by the demands such as type of website, pages required, designs and features. Thus, it is always better to have an idea of the services provided rather than go for the cheapest deal available.

How do I choose the best website development company in Kashmir?

Check their past work / portfolio and test some of the sites they have built. Ask them about their approach to mobile friendly designs, SEO, speed optimization, security, hosting, maintenance and support after launch. Most importantly, find someone who can help you achieve your particular goals instead of just offering a standard website development package.

Is SEO included in website development?

SEO can be included in website development, however, it depends on the developer/agencies. Technical SEO is included in a basic website development package. It covers clean URL structure, HTML headings, sitemaps, responsive design, meta tags, and structured data. Other aspects of SEO like content creation and link building are done separately.

Should You Hire a Local Web Developer in Srinagar?

Hiring a local web developer in Srinagar can be a good choice if your company’s primary market is the Kashmir region since he will have a better perception of the local audience. However, you should not hire someone just because he is based in Srinagar, you should also consider his experience and expertise, past projects, skill set, and level of communication and post-sale support.

How long does it take to create a business website?

It depends on the complexity of the website. We can have an estimate on the time once we see what you want on your website. A business website can be built faster than a shopify website or a custom web application. A professional web developer should always give you a realistic timeline before they start working.

Can a website help my business rank on Google?

A well-developed website can give your business a solid platform for ranking on Google. However, bear in mind that building the website alone cannot ensure your page’s visibility in the search engine results pages (SERPs). Other optimization techniques, such as technical SEO, producing informative content, keyword research, website performance optimization, link building, local SEO, and many others, must be implemented for boosting your rankings.

What questions should I ask a web development company?

Ask them about their past experience, tech stack, costs, timeline, SEO policy, responsive design, hosting options, security measures, revisions policy, and post-launch support options. Moreover, make sure to clarify who owns the domain, hosting, and other services after the project’s completion.

Is WordPress a suitable software for a business?

WordPress can certainly be a good solution for most business sites due to the ease of editing and the opportunity to scale the site up. At the same time, for different projects, specific tools developed individually for the client may be selected. The developer should choose which products to use not based on what they will do for all clients, but based on what is best for the individual business.

Why is mobile-friendly website design so important?

Because many users often surf the web using their smartphones, having a website that is not optimized for smaller screens is a bad idea. This is because such a website would not be convenient to use, and thus, the user would leave the website without contacting the company. A responsive website allows for a better user experience and is easier to navigate on different devices.

Do I need website maintenance after launch?

Website maintenance can include a variety of tasks, which can be beneficial for sites that rely on WordPress, plugins, databases, forms or other updatable systems. It can involve updates, backups, security, performance and technical support among other services. The degree to which it’s required will depend on the type of website you have.

Can I update my own website after it is built?

It depends on what technology it is built upon. Some CMS platforms like WordPress or Shopify allows businesses to manage most of their content without outside help. If you opt for a custom website, you might need to hire a developer to make future changes. You should ask what level of control over their website will they have after launch.

What makes a website professional?

A professional website is a website that is easy to navigate, has a good performance, is mobile-friendly and visually attractive. It should tell the visitor what the company is about, offer and how to contact them at the same time provide a pleasant and safe experience. It is important for a website to look good but it should be useful, easy and quick to use too.
